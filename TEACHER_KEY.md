## Set 2
1. You won't be able to distinguish fact from fiction
2. unless you develop your critical thinking.

6. if we studied the science of electromagnetic waves.

9. You can inspire others with your original ideas
10. if you create a unique final product.
11. If people didn't have access to the internet,
12. they couldn't communicate globally in seconds.
13. Democracy will be in serious danger
14. unless we stop the spread of disinformation.
15. If I were a leader during the French Revolution,
16. I might fight for the ideals of Liberty.
17. You will become just another "thumbs-up" follower
18. unless you stop and reflect on what you see.
19. Migratory birds could not travel such long distances
20. if they didn't use clever energy-saving strategies.

22. we wouldn't have modern telecommunications.
23. You can exercise your freedom of choice
24. if you verify the reliability of information.
25. Unless the population is well-informed,
26. public health missions will face many challenges.
27. If social media didn't exist,
28. our daily lives might be much more private.
29. People will respect your dignity
30. unless you act without moral values.

33. The economy would grow much slower
34. if transport systems were not efficient.
35. Unless we respect the rights of others,
36. we will never live in a truly free society.
37. If you were a predator like a tiger,

39. You won't understand history
40. unless you study the context of past revolutions.
41. If the 18th-century bourgeoisie hadn't innovated,

44. if we apply scientific knowledge correctly.
